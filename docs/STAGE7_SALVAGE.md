# Certificate-aware Stage-7 salvage

Stage-7 preserves literal board connectivity as the terminal authority. The
elementary certificate and physical realizer are restricted mechanisms for
audited bridge patterns; they are not a general H-search implementation.

For certificate-producing PUCT games, the retained policy prefix stops at the
first valid certificate. Aggregate salvage counts are published in
`results/stage7-prefix-salvage-summary-v1.json`; raw games and rows remain
private generated data.
