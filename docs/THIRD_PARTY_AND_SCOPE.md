# Scope and provenance

The original University of Manchester COMP34111 Group 49 coursework is
historical context only. It is not copied wholesale here, and the independent
reconstruction should not be represented as coursework submission work.

KataHex, Benzene and Neurobenzene were independently inspected as third-party
references. Their source trees and local patched clones are not distributed
in this repository. board.py records a behavior-preserving port of the
audited KataHex virtual detector; the final publication review should retain
the applicable attribution/notice decision.
